Refund Policy

This is a general-purpose starting point — adjust the specifics (timeframes, percentages) to match how you actually want to run things, and consider having it reviewed. Last updated: August 2026.

1. Before Work Begins

If you've paid for a request and we haven't started work yet, you're entitled to a full refund. Contact us as soon as possible to cancel.

2. Once Work Has Started

If work is already underway, refunds are considered case by case, based on how much has been completed. As a general guide:

  • Cancelling in the early planning/design stage: partial refund, minus any work already delivered.
  • Cancelling after significant development work has been done: refunds are limited to the value of work not yet completed.
  • Once a project has been fully delivered and approved by you: refunds aren't available, though we'll always work with you to resolve any issues with the delivered work first.

3. If Something's Wrong With the Work

If what we deliver doesn't match what was agreed, tell us — we'll fix it. Refunds for quality issues are considered after we've had a fair opportunity to address the problem.

4. How Refunds Are Paid

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Paystack, and typically take 5–10 business days to reflect, depending on your bank.

5. Requesting a Refund

Email us at info@jrextech.com or use the Contact page with your request details, and we'll respond within one business day.
