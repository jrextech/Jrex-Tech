Privacy Policy

This is a general-purpose template to get you started, not legal advice specific to your business or your obligations under Nigerian data protection law (NDPR). We'd recommend having it reviewed before treating it as final. Last updated: August 2026.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password).
  • Service requests: the details you provide when requesting a service — project type, budget range, description, and any files or notes you share with us.
  • Contact form messages: your name, email, and message when you contact us.
  • Payment information: we don't collect or store your card details ourselves — payments are handled directly by Paystack. We keep a record of the amount, status, and reference number of each transaction.

2. How We Use It

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To review, accept, or decline service requests, and to deliver the work you've requested.
  • To email you about the status of your requests (you can turn these off anytime in Settings) and to reply to messages you send us.
  • To process payments for accepted requests.

3. Who We Share It With

We don't sell your information. We share the minimum necessary with:

  • Paystack — to process card, bank transfer, and USSD payments.
  • Our email provider — to send account and request-status emails.

4. How We Store It

Your data is stored in a database on our hosting provider's servers. Passwords are hashed (not stored in readable form) before being saved. We keep account and request data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You can update your name and email notification preferences anytime in Settings. To request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account, contact us at info@jrextech.com.

6. Cookies

We use your browser's local storage to keep you logged in — this isn't shared with third parties and is cleared when you log out.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll update the date at the top of this page when we do.

8. Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us at info@jrextech.com or through our Contact page.
